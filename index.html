About Us

Who We Are

Five Star Group LLC is a property management company based in Millersburg, Ohio. We manage vacation rental properties in the heart of Ohio's Amish Country, handling everything from guest bookings and property maintenance to hospitality services and on-site guest support.

In addition to property management, we offer direct-to-consumer gift certificates for stays at our managed properties, as well as optional in-room hospitality add-ons such as welcome baskets, firewood bundles, candles, snack packages, and celebration packages — all delivered directly to guests at check-in or during their stay.

We do not sell third-party gift cards, open-loop prepaid products, reloadable stored-value products, or payment services for other businesses.

Policies

Our Policies

Clear and fair policies for all customers.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Vacation rental reservations may be cancelled according to the cancellation policy provided at the time of booking. Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation window and specific property terms. Gift certificates may be refunded within 30 days of purchase if unused. In-room add-on packages may be cancelled for a full refund up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date. All refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Booking & Fulfillment

Vacation rental reservations are confirmed by email with check-in instructions and property access details provided prior to arrival. The lodging service is fulfilled at the physical property during the guest's reserved stay dates. Gift certificates are delivered digitally by email, typically within minutes of purchase. In-room packages and hospitality add-ons are delivered on-site to guests at check-in or during their stay.

Customer Verification

All reservations, gift certificate purchases, and add-on orders go through standard online checkout with payment verification, fraud screening, reservation matching, and manual review when needed. We sell directly to individual guests and do not sell to resellers or third-party distributors.

Important Disclosures

Five Star Group LLC does not sell third-party gift cards, open-loop prepaid products, reloadable stored-value products, or payment services for other businesses. All gift certificates sold are first-party products, valid only for stays, services, and items at properties managed by Five Star Group LLC. Our products are sold directly to individual end customers.
